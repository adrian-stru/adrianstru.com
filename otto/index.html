Otto

Otto is a private, single-user assistant that Adrian Stru runs on a personal computer. It helps keep Adrian's personal finances organized, for example by matching emailed purchase receipts to transactions in Adrian's own budgeting software.

Otto is not a public service. It has one user, no sign-up, and nothing for sale. Nobody else can connect an account to it.

See the privacy policy for what data Otto reads and how it is handled.
